How do you choose RAM for your PC?

DDR4 or DDR5?

The processor and motherboard determine which generation you can use. DDR4 and DDR5 are not interchangeable, even when kit names look similar. Check your motherboard specifications before choosing.

16 GB or 32 GB in total?

Suitable capacity depends on your applications and how many programs you run together. Check their requirements and your PC’s memory usage. More memory does not automatically guarantee better performance.

One module or a kit?

A 32 GB kit consisting of 2 × 16 GB contains two 16 GB modules, not two 32 GB modules. Kit modules are sold together; use the slots recommended in your motherboard manual.

Understanding MT/s

Memory data rates are expressed in megatransfers per second (MT/s). DDR4-3200 advertises 3,200 MT/s. Do not confuse this with clock frequency. An XMP or EXPO profile may be needed depending on the kit; stability depends on the platform.

RAM compatibility: what to check

  • Check the motherboard’s DDR4 or DDR5 generation and UDIMM form factor. These products are for desktop PCs, not laptop SO-DIMM slots.
  • Consult the processor and motherboard’s maximum memory capacity and the QVL for the kit part number. The QVL lists tested kits; absence alone does not prove incompatibility.
  • Consult the manual for slot order, BIOS and memory profile settings. Do not assume the advertised data rate will apply automatically.
  • Check module height under the CPU cooler and any connections or software required for lighting, if the model has it.
  • Avoid mixing different kits without testing stability, even when their stated capacities or data rates are identical.

Frequently asked questions

Is the displayed price a real selling price?

No. All NEXRIG amounts are fictional and only serve the demonstration cart. No sales, stock availability or delivery are offered.

Can you use a DDR5 kit with a DDR4 motherboard?

No. The generation and slot must match. Some processors support both generations, but that does not mean every motherboard accepts both.

Is the advertised data rate guaranteed in my PC?

No. The kit, CPU memory controller, motherboard, BIOS and settings influence the usable data rate. Check manufacturer information for the exact part number before enabling a profile.

Why do the illustrations look similar?

The images are original generic RAM illustrations. They do not show each model’s exact shape, colour or lighting.
