Buying guidance

ATX, Micro-ATX or Mini-ITX

Choose a case for your motherboard. The filter describes the primary format, not an exhaustive supported-board list. Check standoffs and dimensions in the manual.

Component clearance

A front radiator can reduce GPU clearance. Check cooler height, PSU length, drive bays and cable routing. Listed 2022 and 2023 versions do not describe later generations.

Airflow and installation

Compare air intakes and fan mounting locations. Separately check included fans and front-panel connections; no temperature or acoustic performance is promised.

What to check before choosing

Check exact dimensions and revision: motherboard format, GPU length, cooler height, PSU bay and radiators with fans. The primary form factor does not describe every mounting option.

Check documentation for the exact model. The cart does not automatically validate compatibility.

Frequently asked questions

Does the primary format guarantee the entire build?

No. Separately check GPU, cooling, PSU, radiators and accessories.
