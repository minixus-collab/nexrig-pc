Choosing audio equipment

IEMs and in-ear monitors

Compare fit, ear tips and isolation needs. The wired IEMs here use analog audio. Check your device’s socket and removable-cable connectors; two-pin and MMCX connectors are not interchangeable.

Headphones and gaming headsets

Headphones do not necessarily include a microphone. Check mic input, TRS/TRRS plugs and exact-variant accessories. Wireless does not automatically mean Bluetooth, and comfort depends on your use case.

Desktop speakers

Check audio inputs, power and desk space. USB may provide power only. Do not connect passive speakers directly to a DAC line output: they need suitable amplification.

USB and XLR microphones

A USB microphone includes digital conversion. An XLR microphone needs a suitable interface or preamp. Phantom-power requirements depend on the microphone and its instructions; do not apply it automatically to every model.

DACs and amplifiers

A DAC converts digital audio to analog; an amplifier boosts an analog signal. SMSL SU-1 is a DAC only, while Schiit Magni+ is an amp only. Check output level, headphone load and connections. A purchase does not guarantee an audible improvement.

Connections and compatibility

Check audio signal, connectors and cables for the exact model. USB can provide power without carrying sound: Creative Pebble V2 uses an analog audio input. An XLR microphone needs a suitable interface or preamp; a passive XLR-to-USB adapter is not enough. A standalone DAC does not replace a headphone amplifier.

Check the operating system, software, adapters and supplied cables for the exact model. The filter describes the primary signal, not an exhaustive input list. Start listening at a low volume.

Frequently asked questions

Are IEMs gaming headsets?

IEMs are in-ear monitors. Their usefulness depends on fit, isolation and your use case; the name does not guarantee gaming accuracy.

Can a DAC drive any headphones?

No. A standalone DAC may provide a line output without a headphone amplifier. Check the role and outputs of the exact model.

Can I order these products?

No. Prices are fictional, illustrations are generic and the cart cannot place a real order.
