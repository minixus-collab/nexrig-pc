Buying guidance

Model family and SKU

A range name or chassis code does not identify the whole configuration. These pages explicitly describe model families: verify CPU, GPU, RAM, SSD, panel and operating system for the exact SKU.

Workload and mobility

Compare your use case, weight, charger and relevant battery tests. A gaming name guarantees neither FPS, noise nor a particular battery runtime.

Connections and upgrades

Consult the exact configuration’s manual for video outputs, USB-C features and RAM/SSD replacement options. Keyboard layout, software and accessories can vary by region.
