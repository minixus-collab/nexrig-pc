How do you choose a processor for your PC?

Start with your use cases

For gaming, consult tests matching your games, graphics card and resolution. For editing, rendering or compiling, compare results in the software you use. A higher core count does not improve every workload by the same amount.

Evaluate the whole platform

A configuration’s budget is not limited to the CPU: account for the motherboard, RAM and cooling. When upgrading, check which components can be reused before choosing another socket.

Understand the specifications

Cores perform calculations, while hardware threads allow the processor to handle multiple instruction streams. Frequency and cache are other reference points, but are not enough to compare different architectures. Use measurements made under the same conditions.

Compare offers in Morocco

Compare the exact model, new or used condition and box contents. Check whether a cooler is included, the warranty terms and the total cost. NEXRIG does not provide current prices or partner sellers.

Check compatibility before choosing a CPU

  • Socket and motherboard: the socket must match, but that is not enough. Consult the supported processor list for the exact motherboard model.
  • BIOS: check the minimum required version and the manufacturer’s update procedure. An update may be necessary before the new CPU can boot.
  • RAM: use the DDR4 or DDR5 generation supported by the motherboard and processor. These modules use different slots and are not direct replacements for each other.
  • Cooling: check the mounting kit for the socket, case dimensions and thermal recommendations. Do not assume every retail version includes a cooler.
  • Display: a Ryzen 5 5600 or Core i5-12400F requires a separate graphics card. For a processor with integrated graphics, also check the motherboard’s video outputs.
  • Power supply: size the power supply for the entire PC and check the motherboard’s CPU power connectors.

Frequently asked questions about PC processors

AMD Ryzen or Intel Core: which should you choose?

Compare specific models in your games and software. Also consider compatibility with your existing hardware and the cost of the complete platform. The brand alone does not determine the best choice.

Can you install an AM5 processor on an AM4 motherboard?

No. AM4 and AM5 are different sockets. The Ryzen 5 7600 requires a compatible AM5 motherboard and DDR5 memory; it cannot directly replace a Ryzen 5 5600 on its AM4 motherboard.

Can an Intel processor supporting DDR4 and DDR5 use both on the same motherboard?

The Core i5-12400F supports both generations, but the motherboard determines which one to use. Choose RAM matching its slots and specifications; do not mix DDR4 and DDR5.
