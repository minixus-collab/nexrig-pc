SSDs in Morocco: how to choose and compare prices

When choosing an SSD in Morocco, start with PC compatibility, the capacity you need and your workload. Then compare offers for the same exact model. A high advertised speed or low price alone does not identify the best purchase.

SATA or NVMe SSD: check the format first

An SSD stores data in flash memory. Compared with a mechanical hard drive, it can make system startup, application launches and loading faster. The result depends on the hardware and task; an SSD is not a substitute for a backup.

A 2.5-inch SATA SSD generally needs a SATA data cable and suitable power connection. An M.2 SSD mounts in a designated motherboard slot. M.2 describes a form factor, not automatically NVMe: some M.2 drives use SATA, while others use NVMe over PCIe.

Read your PC or motherboard manual to check supported protocols, module length, PCIe lanes and any ports disabled when the slot is occupied. M.2 2280 describes a module 22 mm wide and 80 mm long; that designation alone does not guarantee compatibility.

A newer-generation PCIe SSD can often run at a lower speed on an older platform if the slot and system are compatible. Check both exact models’ documentation. For a laptop, also check clearance, the number of slots and whether storage is replaceable.

Our storage catalogue separates SSDs from HDDs. For a new build, compare motherboards and read their exact documentation before selecting a drive.

How much SSD capacity does a gaming PC need?

List your operating system, applications, games and files. Updates and temporary installations also need space. Game sizes vary, so a capacity cannot promise a fixed number of installed titles.

  • Around 500 GB: may suit a small library if you manage space regularly.
  • 1 TB: a starting point to consider for several games and everyday use.
  • 2 TB or more: worth considering for a larger library, videos or substantial projects.

These are planning examples, not universal requirements. Your operating system may show a different usable capacity because of units, formatting and partitions. Leave space appropriate to your installations and the manufacturer’s recommendations.

Compare more than the maximum transfer speed

Advertised sequential speeds describe particular test conditions. Small-file access, latency and sustained writes can give a different picture. A drive that copies large files quickly does not necessarily deliver a proportional improvement in every game.

Compare independent tests of the same capacity and exact model. Versions within one range can have different components or endurance. Some writes slow after the cache is exhausted; test conditions and available space matter.

Does an SSD increase FPS?

Its most direct effect generally involves loading and data access. It does not guarantee a higher average framerate. Some games require an SSD or may handle resource streaming better with suitable storage: check official requirements. CPU, GPU and RAM remain important for many aspects of performance.

Endurance, warranty and temperature

TBW describes a quantity of writes associated with the model’s specifications or warranty conditions. It is neither a failure date nor a promise that every drive lasts equally long. Compare TBW for the selected capacity, warranty duration and terms, and the manufacturer’s available tools.

Some drives can reduce speed when hot. A heatsink may be useful or recommended, but it must fit your system. Do not stack heatsinks or remove a protective assembly without consulting the manual. A desktop heatsink version may not fit inside a laptop.

How to compare SSD prices in Morocco

NEXRIG does not publish a real market-price survey. Catalogue amounts in MAD are fictional and demonstrate the cart. They are neither commercial offers nor estimates of Moroccan market prices.

To compare real sellers, record offers on the same day and note:

  1. The complete model number, capacity and version with or without a heatsink.
  2. Whether the listing describes the drive as new, used or refurbished.
  3. The total payable amount, including delivery and any stated additional fees.
  4. Advertised availability, invoice, returns and warranty terms.
  5. The seller’s identity and how after-sales support is handled.

Price per TB helps compare capacity, but does not measure compatibility, performance or support quality. Be cautious with listings that omit the exact model number. Check a promotion’s conditions before comparing its amount with another offer.

This guide gives no market-price range without dated, verifiable offers. Use the catalogue to understand specifications; consult sellers for their current prices.

Before installing or replacing an SSD

Back up important files to another device and check that the backup can be read. If your system uses encryption, retain the recovery information you need before making changes. Then choose between a clean operating-system installation and a migration suited to your configuration.

Shut down and unplug the PC, then follow the manufacturer’s installation instructions. Check the form factor, mounting screw or retention mechanism and thermal coverings. Use official tools for firmware updates and back up your data first. Ask a technician if you are unfamiliar with these steps.

Frequently asked questions

Are NVMe and M.2 the same thing?

No. M.2 is a form factor; NVMe is a protocol. Check both the drive and slot’s format and protocol.

Should you buy the fastest SSD?

Not automatically. Compatibility, capacity, performance in your workload and the total price may matter more than a maximum transfer rate.

Can an SSD fail?

Yes. A warranty or health indicator does not protect your files from every kind of loss. Keep independent backups.
