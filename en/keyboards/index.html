Buying guidance

Mechanical or magnetic

A conventional mechanical keyboard closes an electrical contact. The magnetic models here use Hall-effect sensing. Both contain moving parts, but their detection and adjustment options differ.

Size and layout

Compact, TKL and full-size describe key groups here, not regional layout. Check ISO/ANSI, AZERTY/QWERTY, legends and the exact variant before choosing.

Features and switch replacement

Rapid Trigger and actuation settings depend on model, firmware and affected keys. Some Apex Pro models combine switch technologies. A magnetic switch is not a universal replacement for a mechanical switch; also check hot-swap support.

What to check before choosing

Check AZERTY/QWERTY layout, ISO/ANSI variant and supplied keycaps. Magnetic keyboards use Hall-effect sensing; features, affected keys and settings vary by model and firmware.

Check documentation for the exact model. The cart does not automatically validate compatibility.
