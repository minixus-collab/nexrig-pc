Choosing CPU cooling

Air cooler or liquid AIO?

An air cooler dissipates heat through a heatsink and fans. An AIO uses a sealed liquid loop with a pump and radiator. Compare available space, installation, noise and tests matching your processor.

Socket and mounting kit

Check supported sockets, cooler revision and supplied mounting hardware. A newer platform kit may not be included in older packaging. NH-L9a-AM5 specifically identifies an AM5 variant.

Height, memory and case clearance

For air coolers, check side-panel and RAM clearance. For AIOs, check mounting location, actual radiator dimensions and combined fan thickness. 240, 280 and 360 mm are nominal size classes.

Performance and connections

Temperature depends on CPU, workload, airflow, thermal paste and ambient temperature. Different brands’ TDP ratings are not a universal comparison. Check PWM, pump and RGB connections in the manual.

Compatibility checks

  • Check the compatibility list and package contents for the exact model and revision.
  • Check RAM, motherboard and case clearance; raising a fan can increase total air-cooler height.
  • For an AIO, check radiator, fans, tubing and mounting points. Follow manufacturer instructions for orientation and connections.
  • Do not confuse 12 V RGB with 5 V ARGB connectors. Use only a compatible connection for each plug.
  • Follow instructions for thermal paste and removal of specified transport protections. The cart does not automatically validate installation.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 360 mm AIO always better than an air cooler?

No. Compare tests, CPU workload, noise and case constraints. Type or nominal size does not guarantee a temperature.

Can I mount any cooler on AM5?

No. Check the exact model, supported socket and mounting kit. The NH-L9a-AM5 model here is not an Intel variant.

Can I order these products?

No. The cart is a demonstration, prices are fictional and images are generic illustrations rather than model photographs.
