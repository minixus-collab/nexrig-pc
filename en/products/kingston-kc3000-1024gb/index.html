Compatibility to check

This NVMe SSD needs an M.2 slot supporting NVMe and the 2280 length (22 × 80 mm). Check the slot’s PCIe generation and lane count. A SATA-only M.2 slot is not suitable. An older PCIe connection can limit throughput; consult the manual and NVMe boot support.

Manufacturer-advertised and system-reported capacity can differ because of units and formatting. Check heatsink or cooling requirements for the exact model. This catalogue makes no measured speed or guaranteed endurance claims. Keep an independent backup of important files.
