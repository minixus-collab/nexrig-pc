Buying guidance

Dimensions and fit

Compare seat width, depth, height and adjustments with your body and desk. TITAN Evo Small, Regular and XL are separate variants; check manufacturer recommendations.

Fabric or synthetic upholstery

Upholstery variants are named explicitly. Compare maintenance and everyday preferences. Synthetic upholstery is not genuine leather; exact compositions vary by model.

Assembly and use

Consult limits, assembly instructions, flooring and adjustments for the exact model. A gaming chair is not a medical device and does not guarantee pain relief.

What to check before choosing

Compare seat dimensions, adjustments and manufacturer limits for the exact size and upholstery variant. A gaming label guarantees neither universal comfort nor medical benefits.

Check documentation for the exact model. The cart does not automatically validate compatibility.
