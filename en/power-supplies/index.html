Choosing a power supply

How much power do you need?

Consider the processor, graphics card and other components, together with the GPU manufacturer’s recommendation. Allow suitable headroom for load spikes and future changes. Wattage alone does not guarantee quality.

Fixed or modular cables?

Non-modular units have permanently attached cables. Fully modular units have detachable cables. This can simplify cable management, but does not make cables interchangeable between models.

ATX form factor and case space

ATX here describes the physical form factor, not an electrical standard revision. Check exact unit length, case bay and cable clearance. A compact case may require SFX.

Efficiency and other specifications

An efficiency certification does not describe all protections, noise or electrical quality. Check the exact model and revision in manufacturer documentation and independent tests.

Compatibility checks

  • Check required CPU EPS and GPU connectors, supplied cables and the graphics card manufacturer’s recommendation.
  • For newer GPU connectors, verify explicit model compatibility and follow the connection and cable-bending instructions.
  • Do not reuse modular cables from another power supply without manufacturer confirmation, even if the plugs appear identical.
  • Check supported mains voltage, suitable power cord, ventilation and case clearance. Do not open the unit.

Frequently asked questions

Does a 750 W PSU constantly consume 750 W?

No. Rated power describes output capacity. Input consumption depends on system load and efficiency.

Can I reuse my old modular cables?

Only if the manufacturer confirms compatibility for the exact models. PSU-side connectors are not universal.

Are the prices real offers?

No. Prices are fictional, pictures are generic illustrations and the cart cannot place a real order.
