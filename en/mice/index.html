Buying guidance

Shape and grip

Compare your hand size and palm, claw or fingertip grip. Model names and DPI numbers cannot guarantee comfort or precision for every user.

Wired or wireless

The filter describes the primary connection. Wireless does not necessarily mean Bluetooth. Check receiver, USB mode, charging and package contents for the exact variant.

Software and buttons

Check supported operating systems, saved settings and software features. Do not infer latency, battery life or polling rate from a range name.

What to check before choosing

Check size, shape, grip and operating-system compatibility. Wireless does not automatically mean Bluetooth: check exact connection modes, supplied receiver and charging options.

Check documentation for the exact model. The cart does not automatically validate compatibility.
