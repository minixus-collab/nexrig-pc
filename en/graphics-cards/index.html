How do you choose a graphics card for your PC?

Start with your monitor and games

Note your monitor’s resolution (1080p, 1440p or 4K), refresh rate and the games you play. Higher resolution generally increases the graphics workload. Compare tests at the same resolution and settings.

Understand video memory

VRAM stores textures and other data used by the GPU. Requirements vary by game, resolution and settings. Do not choose a card solely by its number of gigabytes.

Check your creative software

For video editing, 3D or streaming, check your software’s requirements: GPU acceleration, supported codecs and memory needs. The right choice may differ from a PC used only for gaming.

Compare offers in Morocco

Compare the same model across several sellers. Check the total price, warranty terms, new or used condition and return policy. NEXRIG does not provide current prices or a list of partner sellers.

Compatibility points to check

  • Case: compare the card’s length, height and thickness with the available space, accounting for fans and radiators.
  • Power supply: consult the card manufacturer’s recommendations and check the wattage and connectors needed for your entire PC.
  • Motherboard: check the available PCI Express slot, the space around it and the manufacturer’s compatibility guidance.
  • Monitor: check the card’s HDMI or DisplayPort outputs, the monitor’s inputs and a cable suitable for your intended resolution and refresh rate.
  • Processor and system: performance depends on the complete configuration. Also check driver support for your operating system.

Frequently asked questions about graphics cards

NVIDIA or AMD: which graphics card should you choose?

Compare specific models rather than brands alone. Consider performance in your games or software, power consumption, required features and the cost of the model available from the seller.

Is 8 GB of video memory enough for gaming?

It depends on the game, resolution and graphics settings. Some 1080p uses remain suitable for 8 GB, while heavier textures may require more memory. Consult recent tests matching your use case.
