Choosing a PC monitor

Full HD, QHD or 4K?

Full HD here means 1920 × 1080 pixels, QHD means 2560 × 1440 and 4K UHD means 3840 × 2160. Higher resolution increases graphics workload in games. Choose for your use case, GPU and viewing distance.

Refresh rate and frames per second

Refresh rate describes screen updates; FPS describes frames produced by the PC. A 240 Hz monitor does not guarantee 240 FPS. Listed rates are nominal, excluding separate overclock modes.

IPS and VA

Panel type is a comparison point, not a universal guarantee of colour, contrast or response. Consult measurements for the exact model at realistic settings rather than relying on a marketed response-time number.

Screen size and ergonomics

Choose a diagonal size for your desk and viewing distance. Check dimensions, stand adjustments and VESA support before planning a monitor arm. The exact model and revision matter.

Connections and compatibility

  • Check supported modes for each HDMI or DisplayPort input. Maximum resolution and refresh rate can differ between ports.
  • Check GPU output, cable capability and adapter or dock limits. A laptop must also support the selected display mode.
  • Select the resolution and refresh rate in your operating system and check monitor settings.
  • Separately verify VRR, HDR, USB-C, speakers and included accessories for the exact model.
  • Game performance depends on the GPU, CPU and settings. The cart does not automatically verify these constraints.

Frequently asked questions

Does a 144 Hz monitor always display 144 FPS?

No. The monitor and PC have different roles. Monitor refresh rate does not guarantee the frames rendered by a game.

Can any cable handle high-refresh 4K?

No. Check the mode supported by both devices and the capabilities of the cable, adapters and port used.

Are these prices real sales offers?

No. Prices are fictional, the cart simulates a selection and illustrations are not model photographs.
