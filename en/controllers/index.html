Choosing a PC controller

Compare grip, button layout and connection mode. A wired controller uses a cable; a wireless model may use Bluetooth or a dedicated receiver. Your game’s controller support also matters.

Compatibility and accessories to check

Check Windows support, drivers, firmware and the game’s XInput/DirectInput or Steam Input modes. Bluetooth is not Xbox Wireless. Advanced DualSense features depend on the game and may require USB. Check whether the required cable, receiver or adapter is included; universal compatibility is not guaranteed.
