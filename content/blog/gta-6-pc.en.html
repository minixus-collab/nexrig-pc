GTA 6 on PC: release date and system requirements explained

Looking for a GTA 6 PC release date or the hardware needed to play? This guide separates announcements to check with Rockstar from preparation ideas. An estimate is not an official requirement or a performance guarantee.

No GTA 6 PC date or official PC requirements are confirmed by the information used for this article. Check Rockstar’s latest announcements before purchasing hardware solely for this game.

When will GTA 6 release on PC?

The supplied console schedule is 19 November 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; check the official GTA VI page to confirm it remains current. This is not a PC release date.

A console release date is not a PC release date. Platforms and timing need confirmation in an official announcement. Check Rockstar’s GTA VI page and official news for current information.

Can previous games predict the date?

GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 received PC releases after their initial console versions. This precedent cannot establish GTA 6’s schedule. A late-2027 or later prediction remains speculation, not an announcement or a guaranteed earliest date.

What PC specs will GTA 6 need?

Without published and verified PC requirements, claiming minimum, recommended or “4K” hardware would be misleading. Needs will depend on the engine, optimization, settings, resolution and frame-rate target.

GTA V Enhanced is context, not GTA 6’s requirements

Another game’s requirements do not automatically apply to GTA 6. GTA V Enhanced can illustrate the categories to check: operating system, CPU, RAM, GPU and storage. Consult its current requirements through Rockstar Support before using specific figures. GTA V Legacy and GTA V Enhanced are also different versions.

RTX 5060, RTX 5070, RX 9070 or RTX 5080: unvalidated possibilities

These models can belong on a personal comparison shortlist. Assigning them to 1080p, 1440p or 4K GTA 6 performance here would lack game benchmark evidence. We do not present them as GTA 6 recommendations.

The same applies to 32 GB RAM and an NVMe SSD: they are possible choices for a modern build, not established requirements for this game. A requirement for an SSD does not automatically mean NVMe is required.

How to prepare without buying blindly

  1. Inventory your PC: exact CPU and GPU models, RAM capacity, power supply and available storage.
  2. Define your goal: native screen resolution, preferred settings and frame-rate target.
  3. Wait for official requirements: distinguish minimum from recommended and check their stated conditions.
  4. Consult PC tests when available: compare your CPU/GPU combination, resolution and settings, distinguishing native rendering, upscaling and frame generation.
  5. Check compatibility: dimensions, connectors, power, platform and operating-system version before upgrading.

GTA 6 PC questions

Can my PC already be declared compatible?

Not based on this article. Official requirements are needed, and comparable tests of the PC version are necessary to evaluate smoothness.

Can a recent card be promised to deliver 60 FPS?

Not without game data at specified settings and resolution. The card’s name or generation alone is insufficient.

Should I buy a PC solely for GTA 6?

Wait for verified information if this game is your only goal. If buying now, choose for current games and applications rather than a promise of future performance.
